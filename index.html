Foresight — AI Meeting Notes

The privacy-first on-device AI meeting assistant for Android. Real-time transcription, structured executive briefs, action items, and local knowledge RAG — with zero audio or transcripts sent to the cloud.

Read Privacy Policy Data Deletion Portal GitHub Repository

🔒 100% On-Device AI

All transcription, summarization, and vector search run locally using Google LiteRT-LM and open Gemma models. Your sensitive meeting conversations never leave your phone.

🎙️ Real-Time Dual Capture

Transcribes ambient microphone speech and internal conference audio via MediaProjection. Features a 4-line floating HUD overlay for seamless active meeting awareness.

⚡ Instant Action Items

Automatically distills spoken discussions into categorized action items, key insights, and structured executive briefs immediately upon meeting conclusion.

🧠 Semantic Knowledge RAG

Import Google Drive slide decks and meeting documents into an on-device vector database powered by EmbeddingGemma. Ask questions and cite real sources instantly.

🛡️ Zero-PII Telemetry

Release health and crash diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics are strictly scrubbed of all transcripts, tokens, emails, and notes. Opt-out at any time in Settings.

🗑️ Complete Data Erasure

One-tap in-app data reset purges all 17 database tables, vector indices, and downloaded models. Plus web-based deletion request portal for uninstalled users.
