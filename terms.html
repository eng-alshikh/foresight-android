2. Description of the Application

Foresight — AI Meeting Notes is an on-device personal productivity tool designed to capture meeting speech, transcribe spoken dialogue in real time, generate meeting briefs, extract action items, and provide semantic retrieval over user-imported documents and past notes.

In version 1.0, all AI speech transcription, LLM reasoning, and vector similarity retrieval operate exclusively on-device. No audio, transcripts, or notes are transmitted to remote cloud AI servers.
