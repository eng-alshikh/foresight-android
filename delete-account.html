Account & Data Deletion

Complete guide and web request portal for deleting your Foresight account, meeting data, and stored models.

Google Play Data Safety & Deletion Compliance: In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and Account Deletion requirements, Foresight provides both an immediate in-app deletion mechanism and this web-based deletion portal for users who have uninstalled the application.

1. Understanding Foresight's On-Device Data Storage

Foresight — AI Meeting Notes (com.xenolabs.foresight) is engineered with a strict 100% on-device architecture. Unlike cloud-based meeting recorders, Foresight does not maintain centralized servers storing your meeting audio, transcripts, notes, or knowledge documents.

All data generated while using the application resides exclusively in encrypted, app-private storage on your Android device. As a result, deleting your data from your device permanently and irreversibly purges your data with zero retention on remote servers.

2. Immediate In-App Deletion Walkthrough

If you currently have the Foresight app installed on your device, you can instantaneously delete all account associations, meeting transcripts, database records, and downloaded models:

  1. Open the Foresight — AI Meeting Notes app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon from the navigation rail or side menu.
  3. Scroll down to the Data & Storage Management section.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Delete all user data and models".
  5. A confirmation modal will appear. Tap "Delete Everything" to confirm.
What happens during in-app deletion:
  • All 17 app database tables (notes, meeting transcripts, insights, graph edges, contacts, clusters, labels) are completely truncated and dropped.
  • The local RAG vector embeddings database (RagDocumentsDatabase) and token statistics (TokenUsageDatabase) are destroyed.
  • All Google OAuth tokens stored in the hardware-backed AndroidKeyStore are securely wiped.
  • All downloaded on-device AI model binaries (Gemma, LiteRT-LM) and memory cache files are erased from disk.
  • All user preferences are restored to factory defaults and the app restarts to the initial onboarding screen.

3. Revoking Google Account Permissions

If you linked your Google Account to sync Google Calendar events or index Google Drive attachments, you can revoke Foresight's access at any time directly through Google:

  1. Visit the official Google Third-Party Apps & Services Portal.
  2. Select Foresight — AI Meeting Notes (or Foresight Notes).
  3. Click "Remove Access" or "Delete all connections".

This immediately invalidates all OAuth tokens on Google's servers, preventing any further retrieval of calendar or document metadata even if the application remains on your phone.
